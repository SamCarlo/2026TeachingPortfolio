# Portfolio Summary

The 2026 portfolio is a teaching philosophy made visible.

The philosophy says: master skills before using tools, and when you do use tools, keep them invisible to students. The folder proves it in layers — a fully documented argument essay unit with no AI, a short story unit where AI produced the worksheets but students never knew, and two data tools that put MAP Growth scores in front of teachers and administrators without touching students. Everything points the same direction: computation amplifies the teacher; the teacher amplifies the student.
