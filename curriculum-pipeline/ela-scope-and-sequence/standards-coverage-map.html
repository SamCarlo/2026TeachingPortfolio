Continuity Gaps

Taught in Grades 5-6, not explicitly in the 7-8 chips. These are the vertical-alignment watch-items.
  • RI.5, RI.7, RI.8, RI.9 — informational text structure, multi-source / multimedia integration, evaluating arguments, comparing two texts. The 7-8 plan keeps RI.1-4 and RI.6 but leans literary.
  • RL.9 — comparing genres / a text to its source or historical account.
  • W.6, W.7, W.8 — publishing with technology, sustained research projects, and gathering / assessing sources. Research happens in Grade 7 Q3 but is chipped only as W.9.
  • SL.2, SL.3 — analyzing information across media and evaluating a speaker's argument.

Strengths of the 7-8 Plan

  • Full Language strand (L.1-6) via daily Morning Edit + Just Words, matching 5-6.
  • Foundational RF.2-4 (phonics, syllables, fluency) added through Just Words — a band the 5-6 scope only touches "as needed."
  • Writing 1-5 + 9-10 and RL.1-7 fully carried, with deeper revision cycles.

Suggested moves

Fold RI.7/8/9W.7/8 into the Grade 7 Brain Science research quarter and the Grade 8 Frankenstein/R&J units; add SL.2/3 to the Circle of Knowledge seminars. That closes every outlined cell without new units.
