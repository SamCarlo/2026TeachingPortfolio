ELA 7-8 · Scope & Sequence · 2026-2027 Program Overview

How the Program Works

Every class period carries two instructional engines. The first half of each period drives the core literature and writing unit: guided reading, evidence-based writing, and discussion built on the Core Six strategies. The second half delivers Wilson Just Words, an explicit Tier 2 decoding and spelling curriculum that closes foundational gaps while the literary units push grade-level analysis. A daily Morning Edit warm-up opens each session with hands-on conventions practice.

Anatomy of a Class Period
Opening · ~10 min Morning Edit Students correct a 10-error paragraph drawn from the current unit's world. Daily, cumulative conventions practice.
First Half · Core Unit Literature & Writing Block Guided reading, the weekly assignment, and one-on-one feedback conferences, following the three-session cycle below.
Second Half · Tier 2 Wilson Just Words Multisensory word study: sound drills, syllable concepts, word and sentence reading, and dictation. Sequence on page 7.

The Weekly Three-Session Cycle

  • Session 1 (80 min): Guided reading of the week's text, assignment launched, first feedback conferences begin.
  • Session 2 (80 min): Assignment re-taught, independent work, conferences continue. Most students finish and revise.
  • Session 3 (Fri · 40 min): Catch-up and revision. Finished students move to independent reading journals or extra credit writing.
  • Grading: Initial grades by conference before the weekend; final grades entered the following Session 1.

Assignment Design Commitments

  • Weekly readings are completable in about 25 minutes: roughly 1,000 words in Grade 7 and 1,500 words in Grade 8.
  • Every challenging text ships with a simplified version, a vocabulary scaffold, or an audio recording.
  • Modified and ELL Development versions are part of the standard set, never afterthoughts.
  • Formats repeat across the year, so cognitive load stays on the content, not the directions.

Wilson Just Words Word Study

Just Words is Wilson Language Training's accelerated, multisensory Tier 2 decoding and spelling curriculum for grades 4-12. It runs in the second half of every class period, in both grades, all year. The 14-unit sequence below paces the program across four quarters at roughly two weeks per unit, with flex weeks absorbing reteaching as assessment data demands.

Inside Every Just Words Session
1 · Sound DrillRapid multisensory review of sound-symbol correspondence.
2 · Concept TeachingExplicit instruction in the unit's syllable, spelling, or suffix concept.
3 · Word & Sentence ReadingControlled words, phrases, and high frequency words applied in connected text.
4 · DictationStudents encode sounds, words, and full sentences; spelling made automatic.

Standards Coverage

Just Words carries the foundational reading band so the literature units can stay focused on grade-level analysis.

  • Core: RF.2 phonemic awareness; RF.3 phonics, syllable types, and affixes; RF.4 fluency.
  • Core, grades 7-8: L.2 spelling; L.4 Greek and Latin affixes and roots.
  • Partial: L.1 through sentence dictation; L.6 high frequency words.

Pacing Notes

  • Wilson's full-time model is 14 units over 30 weeks; this plan fits a 36-week year with built-in flex weeks.
  • Unit boundaries move with mastery data from unit tests, not with the calendar.
  • Morning Edit and unit vocabulary recycle Just Words patterns, so word study is reinforced outside the block.
Why word study in grades 7-8. Following the Simple View of Reading, comprehension is the product of word recognition and language comprehension. Just Words carries the word recognition strand for students with decoding and spelling gaps, while the core literature units carry vocabulary, knowledge, and analysis. Together they serve the whole reader, consistent with the Science of Reading principles that ground this curriculum.
